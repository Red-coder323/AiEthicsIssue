AI Ethics Issue

AI has been a very trending and helpful tool nowadays, and is being used in businesses, work, development, and other more. However, despite its advantages, it also has its disadvantages. Let us explore the pros and cons of AI!

Let us first learn its advantages:

1. Helps in education

Students can learn a topic or lesson that is rather confusing or unknown to them. It can quiz them, break down key topics, and even quiz the student to test their knowledge.

2. Reduces human error

Humans can make mistakes or errors when doing tasks, including writing texts, coding programs, and calculations. With AI, it can't eliminate human error entirely, considering AI models can also make mistakes, but it can help reduce it.

3. Always available

Unlike humans, AI can be available all-day round since it does not need sleep, nutrients, and other more that humans need. With this, humans can easily get their answers or immediately do a task that is rather difficult to do alone.

4. Less human bias

Depending on the data fed and trained to an AI model, AI can logically decide rather than focus on feelings and bias, which is very helpful for tasks like evaluation, objective decisions, and content moderation.

5. Does not get tired

Humans can get tired, ill, and be unmotivated to do a repetitive task. AI, on the other hand, can do repetitive tasks without tiring out, which is very helpful for providing reports, monitoring data, processing, and other more.


But, just like what was said before, AI has disadvantages, and let's learn it:

1. Loss in creativity

Some people tend to prompt AI to generate creative work rather than making it their own. This can make people lose creativity and oringinality.

Fix: Sure, not everyone knows how to draw or be creative, but instead of constantly generating from AI, learn from AI to develop creative skills.
2. Overuse and over-reliance

This is a very big and common issue when using AI. Instead of only using it for assistance, some people use it to complete tasks entirely and not confirm for misinformation or mistakes. Students can also abuse it to cheat on academics.

Fix: Use AI only for assistance, not to rely on or complete tasks fully. Parents can also monitor their students to ensure that they don't rely and cheat with AI.
3. Loss of independent thinking

This issue is similar to number 2; some people can lose independent thinking because of their over-reliance on AI. It can also cause loss of focus on students.

Fix: Just like on number 2, AI should only be used for assistance, not to be relied on fully.
